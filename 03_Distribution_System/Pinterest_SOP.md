# Pinterest SOP

## 视觉方向

关键词：

- lifestyle realism
- warm light
- imperfect
- late night parenting
- real product handling

避免：

- 纯 AI 信息图
- 过度干净的电商图
- 夸张医疗承诺
- 只有文字的大图

## 每天 2 条发布结构

### Pin A：生活场景

例子：

- 3AM congestion station
- What stayed on our bedside table
- The cleaning setup we actually used

### Pin B：测试结果

例子：

- We timed 5 aspirators at 3AM
- The fastest one was not the fanciest
- Mold check after 14 days

## Pin 文案模板

Title:

`We timed baby nasal aspirator cleaning at 3AM`

Description:

`Real-world cleaning test from QuietBabyGear. We measured how long each aspirator took to disassemble, rinse, and reset under low-light parent conditions.`

## 30 天发布目标

- 60 条 Pin
- 至少 20 条来自真实测试照片
- 至少 10 条来自 Lab Notes
- 至少 5 条导向 lead magnet
